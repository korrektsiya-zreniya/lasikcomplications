Thank You
=========

**Thank You** to all who have signed the `Petition <http://www.thepetitionsite.com/1/support-petition-to-withdraw-lasik-approval>`_! The petition will remain open to new signatures. 

On August 17th, a 42\ |ndash|\ page PDF document containg the first 505 signatures, together with their comments, was sent by certified mail to the following FDA officials:

1. Margaret A. Hamburg, M.D., Commissioner Food and Drug Administration
2. Gregory O'Connell, U.S. Food & Drug Administration Division of Postmarket Surveillance 
3. Doug Wood, Acting Director, U.S. Food & Drug Administration Division of Postmarket Surveillance 
4. Steven Silverman, U.S. Food & Drug Administration Center for Devices and Radiological Health Office of Compliance
5. David Buckles, Ombudsman, Center for Devices and Radiological Health Food and Drug Administration
6. Jeffrey Shuren, M.D., J.D., U.S. Food & Drug Administration Center for Devices and Radiological Health, Office of the Center Director
7. Director, U.S. Food & Drug Administration Center for Devices and Radiological Health Office of Device Evaluation, Division of Ophthalmic, Neurological, and ENT Devices 

Below are return receipts confirming delivery.
