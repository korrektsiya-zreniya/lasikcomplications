Dr. Waxler's MSNBC Interview Video
==================================

This MSNBC interview took place February 18\ :sup:`th`\ , 2011. Morris Waxler, PhD, former Chief of the FDA branch responsible for reviewing LASIK
clinical trials, filed a petition with the FDA calling for immediate withdrawal of LASIK devices and asking for a LASIK public health advisory. In this
interview Dr Waxler explains why. He also intends "to submit CONFIDENTIAL information on these matters [#]_ separately to FDA’s Office of Criminal
Investigation."
