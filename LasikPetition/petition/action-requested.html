I. Action Requested¶

I, Morris Waxler [cit5], am the former Branch Chief in charge of FDA approvals of LASIK devices between 1996–2000. I request FDA commissioner, Dr. Margaret Hamburg, take the following actions:

  • Withdraw FDA approval (PMA) for all LASIK devices
  • Issue a Public Health Advisory with a voluntary recall of LASIK devices
